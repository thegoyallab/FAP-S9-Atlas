# Evolutionary table notes — final v2

## Table: Evolutionary sister-pair comparison
Sequence identity is derived from the curated human protein alignments. Expression-profile correlations are final values from the complete 1,086-dataset Census extraction, calculated across 1,431 robust tissue-by-cell-type strata shared by all nine S9 genes. The expression metric is median donor log2 CPM. Uncertainty is represented by 95% tissue-cluster bootstrap intervals from 5,000 replicates. FAP-DPP4 has rho = 0.1354 (95% interval 0.0740-0.2104), DPP8-DPP9 0.6309 (0.5703-0.6958), DPP6-DPP10 0.7847 (0.6100-0.8735), and PREP-PREPL 0.5786 (0.4515-0.6783).

## Table: FAP-DPP4 boundary validation
Genomic architecture summarizes the current reference annotations and local sequence analyses. Absence of a second locus in coelacanth is interpreted conservatively; secondary loss cannot be excluded absolutely. Residue states refer to the position homologous to human FAP A657. Newt and caecilian entries provide sequence-level validation where locus-level synteny was not fully audited.

## Table: FAP-DPP4 topology tests
The minimal lineage-specific constraint requires the biologically relevant duplication structure while allowing uncertain basal single-copy taxa to arrange freely. The fully species-ordered lineage-specific tree is not used as the primary hypothesis because exact basal placement is less stable. The forced ancient-orthology model is rejected by sequence likelihood despite requiring fewer duplication/loss events under simple reconciliation parsimony.

## Table: DPP6-DPP10 catalytic-site ancestral states
Posterior probabilities are taken from the current maximum-likelihood ancestral-state reconstruction at the DPP4-S630-equivalent position. The vertebrate DPP6/DPP10 ancestral node favors Ser (0.80255) over Ala (0.18624). The exact intermediate path to the extant DPP6 Asp and DPP10 Gly states is therefore not assigned.
