# Interpretation boundaries

- An unmeasured gene is not treated as a biological zero.
- Detection-defined FAP/DPP4 states do not establish lineage direction.
- Transcript detection does not establish surface-protein abundance or enzymatic activity.
- Cross-study healthy-versus-cancer pooling is not the principal breast disease-effect test.
- Spatial affinity is not cell identity; Visium spots are multicellular.
- The epithelial-interface hypothesis was tested and was not supported.
- Sequence divergence is not interpreted as causing expression divergence.
